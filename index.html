Explore Auroville

Tucked into the Universal Farm House layout in Edayanchavadi — quiet at home, minutes from everything Auroville.

~10 min

Matrimandir & Visitor Centre

Auroville’s golden sphere and viewing point, plus the Visitor Centre’s boutiques, cafés and exhibitions.

5–15 min

Auroville Cafés & Boutiques

Bakeries, wood-fired pizza, artisan shops and quiet garden cafés scattered through the green belt.

On your doorstep

Edayanchavadi Village

A living Tamil village at the edge of Auroville — temples, markets and everyday local colour.

~25 min

Pondicherry White Town

French-quarter streets, the Promenade beach, and heritage cafés — an easy evening trip.
